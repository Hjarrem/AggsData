How this benchmark is calculated

This benchmark is a model output, not a simple average of the orders below. Each historical order is weighted by:

  • Distance — closer deliveries count more (semivariogram model; range follows your search radius).
  • Recency — newer orders count more (~2-year half-life; orders under a year old are boosted).
  • Volume — typical market orders are favored; very small and very large jobs are discounted.

Historical prices are escalated to today at 3%/yr before weighting. The range is ±1 standard deviation of comparable delivered prices — it widens when nearby prices disagree or data is thin.
